# Ranged Pumps 9minecraft Mod - Extract Fluids Safely From Above

Ranged Pumps 9minecraft Mod adds a single pump block that extracts water or lava from any pool within its range - no complicated setup, no scaffolding down to fluid level, and no need to place the pump right at the edge of the source. Place the pump anywhere above a body of water or lava, connect a pipe or tank to its output, and it will draw fluid from the pool below into your storage system.

## What this build is

An unofficial rebuild of **RefinedMods**'s original work, kept running on current releases by 9Minecraft Studios. Not affiliated with the original author and not endorsed by them.
